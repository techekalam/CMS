Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Capstone Management System.

Account & Login

How do I log into the system?

Enter your registered email address and password on the login page, then select the login option.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password recovery option on the login page. If you are still unable to access your account, contact the support team.

How can I change my password?

Open your account settings and select the change password option.

Projects

How do I view my project?

Open the Projects section from your dashboard to view the project information available to your account.

How do I submit a project document?

Open the appropriate project submission section and follow the instructions provided for uploading your document.

What should I do if my document will not upload?

Check the file requirements and your internet connection. If the problem continues, contact the support team.

Supervision

How can I contact my supervisor?

Use the supervision or project communication features provided by the system to contact your assigned supervisor.

How can I view my project progress?

Open your project dashboard to view the available project progress information.

Assessments

Where can I view my assessment results?

Open the assessment or results section of the system to view available assessment information.

Why is my assessment not showing?

An assessment may not appear until it has been recorded and made available in the system. If you believe a result should already be available, contact your supervisor or support.

Can I request help about an assessment?

Yes. If you have an issue concerning an assessment, submit a support request with details of the problem.

Technical Support

The system is not loading. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and try refreshing the page. If the problem continues, contact the support team.

How do I report a technical problem?

Open the Contact Support page and provide a description of the problem so the support team can investigate it.

Still Need Help?

If you cannot find an answer to your question, you can contact the support team by submitting a support request.
